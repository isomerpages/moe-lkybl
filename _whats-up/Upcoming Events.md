<p>Bilingualism comes alive through meaningful, engaging experiences. Our
upcoming programmes continue to bring together partners, youth, and the
community to explore language in dynamic and creative ways.</p>
<h4>Bilingual Buzz: Creator Hackathon</h4>
<p>In collaboration with Republic Polytechnic</p>
<p>The Bilingual Buzz Creator Hackathon brings together youth creators to
explore how bilingualism can be expressed in the digital age. Participants
will ideate, design, and produce content that reflects authentic bilingual
experiences, leveraging storytelling, media, and emerging technologies.</p>
<p>Through mentorship and hands-on creation, this hackathon aims to empower
young people to become advocates of bilingualism, shaping narratives that
resonate with their peers and wider audiences.</p>
<h4>Echoes of the Forbidden Hill: Bilingual Amazing Race</h4>
<p>In collaboration with Cinewav and National Parks Board</p>
<p>Set against the rich historical landscape of Fort Canning, this immersive
Amazing Race experience blends storytelling, exploration, and language
learning. Participants will journey through a series of interactive checkpoints,
guided by location-based audio narratives delivered through Cinewav’s platform.</p>
<p>As teams navigate the trails, they will encounter stories that weave together
heritage and bilingual expression, encouraging them to listen, speak, and
think across languages in a meaningful context.</p>
<hr>
<p>Through these initiatives, LKYFB continues to work with partners to create
impactful, real-world opportunities for bilingual learning, particularly
for youth. We invite you to stay connected and be part of these exciting
journeys.</p>
