<p>Bilingualism comes alive through meaningful, engaging experiences. Our
upcoming programmes continue to bring together partners, youth, and the
community to explore language in dynamic and creative ways.</p>
<h4><strong>Echoes of The Forbidden Hill by Cinewav | 30 August 2026</strong></h4>
<p>National Day may be over, but the spirit it ignites - pride in Singapore’s
identity, respect for its many cultures, and the bonds that hold its people
together -does not have to end on 9 August.
<br>This month, Singaporeans have one more chance to celebrate what makes
this nation extraordinary. Echoes of the Forbidden Hill: A Bilingual Amazing
Race Experience is a free, immersive bilingual amazing race where teams
race at Fort Canning Park on Sunday, <strong>30 August 2026, from 7:30PM to 10:00PM</strong>,
a fitting final chapter to a month defined by national pride.
<br>Organised by homegrown audio-tech company Cinewav in collaboration with
the National Parks Board (NParks), Fort Canning Park and Lee Kuan Yew Fund
for Bilingualism, and sponsored by AirAsia Move, the event is a love letter
to Singapore’s multicultural identity - one that participants do not just
observe, but actively live.</p>

<h4><strong>Bilingual Buzz Creator Hackathon 2026 by Republic Polytechnic | 8 &amp; 9 September 2026</strong></h4>
<p>The <strong>Bilingual Buzz Creator Hackathon 2026</strong> is an action-packed,
two-day creative challenge on 8 and 9 September 2026 where Republic Polytechnic
students transform real campus experiences into compelling bilingual short-form
videos. Through hands-on workshops and exciting content challenges, participants
will sharpen their storytelling and mobile-editing skills, celebrate what
their Mother Tongue means to them, and discover how bilingualism can become
a powerful social and professional advantage. It is time to find your voice,
tell your story and create content that gets people talking!</p>

<h4><strong>Bilingual Eco-Festival by PCF Sparkletots | 12 September 2026</strong></h4>
<p>Presented under LKYFB’s Love Our Languages! Community Series, the Bilingual
Eco-Festival builds on the success of the Fund’s 2025 community engagement
efforts. In partnership with PCF Sparkletots Preschool, the festival will
offer a vibrant bilingual experience for preschoolers and their families.</p>
<p>Through interactive booths, storytelling sessions, stage performances
and gamified activities, children will explore environmental sustainability
while learning and using their Mother Tongue Languages in meaningful, everyday
contexts. The festival brings <strong>Love Our Languages!</strong> to life
by nurturing both an appreciation for our languages and a sense of responsibility
for the natural world.</p>
<p>Aligned with the Singapore Green Plan 2030, the event is expected to engage
between 500 and 800 children and parents. It will be graced by Deputy Prime
Minister Gan Kim Yong and Dr Janil Puthucheary as Guests of Honour.</p>
