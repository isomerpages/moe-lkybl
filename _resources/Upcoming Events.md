<p>Bilingualism comes alive through meaningful, engaging experiences. Our
upcoming programmes continue to bring together partners, youth, and the
community to explore language in dynamic and creative ways.</p>
<p><strong>Echoes of The Forbidden Hill by Cinewav</strong>
</p>
<p>National Day may be over, but the spirit it ignites - pride in Singapore’s
identity, respect for its many cultures, and the bonds that hold its people
together -does not have to end on 9 August.
<br>This month, Singaporeans have one more chance to celebrate what makes
this nation extraordinary. Echoes of the Forbidden Hill: A Bilingual Amazing
Race Experience is a free, immersive bilingual amazing race where teams
race at Fort Canning Park on Sunday, <strong>30 August 2026, from 7:30PM to 10:00PM</strong>,
a fitting final chapter to a month defined by national pride.
<br>Organised by homegrown audio-tech company Cinewav in collaboration with
the National Parks Board (NParks), Fort Canning Park and Lee Kuan Yew Fund
for Bilingualism, and sponsored by AirAsia Move, the event is a love letter
to Singapore’s multicultural identity - one that participants do not just
observe, but actively live.</p>
