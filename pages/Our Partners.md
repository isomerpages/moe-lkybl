<h2>Our Partners</h2>
<p>At the Lee Kuan Yew Fund for Bilingualism (LKYFB), our work is made meaningful
through partnership. Bilingualism is not built in isolation. It is nurtured
through shared effort across homes, schools, communities, and industries.</p>
<p>Over the years, the Fund has worked closely with a diverse network of
partners who share a common belief: that bilingualism should be lived,
experienced, and sustained across everyday contexts.</p>
<h4>A Collective Effort</h4>
<p>Our partners include educators, content creators, community organisations,
and industry collaborators. Together, we co-create programmes, develop
resources, and design experiences that bring bilingualism to life for children,
youths, and families.</p>
<p>These partnerships allow us to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Translate policy into practice through ground-up initiatives</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop meaningful, engaging bilingual content across platforms</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reach learners across different age groups and contexts</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Foster environments where languages are used naturally and confidently</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Partnerships in Action</h4>
<p>From digital campaigns to on-the-ground programmes, our collaborations
reflect the evolving ways in which bilingualism can be supported.</p>
<p>We have worked with:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Institutes of Higher Learning to empower youths as bilingual advocates</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Media and digital partners to create relatable, youth-centric content</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Community organisations to bring bilingual experiences into everyday spaces</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Educators and specialists to develop research-informed resources for early
learners</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Each partnership is guided by a shared commitment to relevance, impact,
and sustainability.</p>
<h4>Growing Together</h4>
<p>As the bilingual landscape continues to evolve, so too does the nature
of our partnerships. We actively seek collaborators who are keen to explore
new ideas, innovate with purpose, and contribute to a broader ecosystem
that supports bilingualism in meaningful ways.</p>
<p>Whether through programmes, content, or community initiatives, our partners
play a vital role in shaping how bilingualism is experienced by the next
generation.</p>
<h4>Partner With Us</h4>
<p>We welcome like-minded organisations and individuals to journey with us.</p>
<p>If you are interested in partnering with LKYFB, we invite you to reach
out and explore how we can work together to support bilingualism in Singapore.</p>
