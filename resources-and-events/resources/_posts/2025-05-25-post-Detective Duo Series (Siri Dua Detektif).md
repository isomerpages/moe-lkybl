<p>Join young detectives Zaaim and Hulu as they embark on exciting missions
filled with mystery, problem-solving, and adventure! Whether they’re navigating
a bustling wet market to uncover a secret recipe or tracking Grandpa’s
Sunday morning routine with a compass and map, the Detective Duo use their
wit, teamwork, and curiosity to crack every case. Through clever riddles
and thrilling discoveries, this series takes readers on fun-filled journeys
that celebrate family, exploration, and the joy of uncovering hidden clues
in everyday life.</p>
