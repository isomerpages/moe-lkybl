<p>Silat Warisan Budaya Saya by Isiah Majid is a compelling book series that
celebrates the rich tapestry of Malay culture through the dynamic lens
of <em>pencak silat</em>. Centred on the journey of Dina and Bahqer, two
young students from a local silat club, the series invites readers to step
into a world where tradition, language, and movement converge.</p>
<p>As Dina and Bahqer deepen their practice, they uncover not just martial
techniques but cultural gems—phrases like <em>“mengambur anak harimau”</em> and <em>“tangkapan kapis”</em>—terms
that roll off the tongues of seasoned practitioners yet sound like poetic
riddles to the uninitiated. These expressions are more than just combat
instructions; they reflect values, metaphors, and the worldview embedded
in Malay heritage.</p>
<p>Through their story, Isiah Majid opens a portal into the world of <em>silat</em> as
more than a martial art. It becomes a vessel for language, a keeper of
stories, and a living tradition that continues to shape identity and inspire
generations. <em>Silat Warisan Budaya Saya</em> is a stirring reminder of
how culture is best learnt not just through books or classrooms, but through
the heart, body, and spirit.</p>
