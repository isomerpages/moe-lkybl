<p>தமிழும் நானும் (Tamilum Naanum) is a delightful series of 13 thematic
early readers designed to introduce preschoolers to foundational Tamil
vocabulary in a fun, engaging, and accessible way. Presented through animated
videos, read-along flipbooks, printable eBooks, and downloadable colouring
pages, the series invites young learners to explore the Tamil language
at their own pace.</p>
<p>With charming characters and vibrant illustrations, each theme brings
everyday words to life, helping little ones build confidence and curiosity
in their mother tongue. Whether watching, reading, or colouring, children
are encouraged to immerse themselves in a joyful learning experience.</p>
<p><em>தமிழும் நானும்</em> was conceptualised by Abhi Krish, founder of Nool
Monsters, with art and animation by the talented Vasu (@vasuscribbles),
and captivating voiceovers by the beloved storyteller Janaki Sabesh (@maajanaki).</p>
<p>This project was made possible with the support of the Lee Kuan Yew Fund
for Bilingualism.</p>
<p>Happy learning and discovering—one word at a time!</p>
