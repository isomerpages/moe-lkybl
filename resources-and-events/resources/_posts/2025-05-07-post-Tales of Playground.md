<p>游乐场传说 Tales of Playground is a whimsical bilingual picture book series
that brings local playgrounds to life through imaginative storytelling
and vibrant illustrations. Each tale explores the hidden magic of Singapore’s
iconic play spaces, inviting young readers to see familiar structures as
portals to adventure, friendship, and wonder.</p>
<p>Told in both English and Chinese, the series not only sparks creativity
but also supports early bilingual development by encouraging children to
connect meaningfully with both languages.</p>
<p>Supported by @bilingualismsg and co-published by @worldscientificedu and
Vitamin M, <em>Tales of Playground</em> is a celebration of language, play,
and the power of stories rooted in our everyday surroundings.</p>
<p>Let the adventures begin—right at the playground!</p>
