<p>In partnership with the Lee Kuan Yew Fund for Bilingualism, the YCM –
Bilingualism grant offers additional funding for youths to promote bilingualism.
If you are keen to promote bilingualism and encourage the usage of both
English and Mother Tongue languages in daily lives, you may be funded up
to $6,000 or $8,000 to kickstart your project!
<br>
<br>For more information about the YCM – Bilingualism, please visit <a href="https://go.gov.sg/ycm-bilingualism-factsheet" class="focus-visible:bg-utility-highlight focus-visible:text-base-content-strong focus-visible:decoration-transparent focus-visible:shadow-focus-visible focus-visible:outline-0 focus-visible:transition-none focus-visible:hover:decoration-transparent outline-none outline-0" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a>. Interested
and eligible applications can apply to the YCM – Bilingualism via the YCM
grant on the <a href="https://oursggrants.gov.sg/grants/nycycm/instruction" class="focus-visible:bg-utility-highlight focus-visible:text-base-content-strong focus-visible:decoration-transparent focus-visible:shadow-focus-visible focus-visible:outline-0 focus-visible:transition-none focus-visible:hover:decoration-transparent outline-none outline-0" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank"><u>OurSG Grants Portal</u></a>.</p>
