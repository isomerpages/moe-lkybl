<p><strong>Before Applying: Important Points To Note</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Applicants are required to source for their own <strong>language experts or consultants</strong> to
ensure suitability and quality of projects. Please include the resumes
of core team members in your application submission. Examples of core team
members with key roles: <strong>language consultant, writer, illustrator, </strong>etc.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The approved funding amount for each project will depend on the nature
and scope of the project, as well as the availability of funds.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Applicants are advised to keep the Fund informed if he/she intends to
seek co-funding support from other organisations/agencies.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Key Considerations</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><u>Events and Programmes </u>
<br>Examples of event/programme types:
<br>- Plays/dramas
<br>- Booths/exhibitions
<br>- Competitions
<br>- Social Media Campaigns</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><u>Teaching and Learning resources </u>
<br>These resources are encouraged to reflect Singapore’s local context and
culture. Examples of resource formats:
<br>- Print
<br>- Digital Media
<br>- Non-digital Media (Games and Teaching Aids)
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Key Considerations</strong>
</p>
<p>The Fund is open to individuals and organisations/institutions with the
relevant experience and expertise to implement the project.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>All submitted proposals should promote the learning of:
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp; - English Language (EL) and any one, two or three
official Mother Tongue Languages</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The Application Form and Proposed Budget should be completed in the English
language and submitted via FormSG.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Only proposals with a completed Application Form will be evaluated for
funding consideration. The form should include information on the specific
deliverables, time-line, milestones and project costing.
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Eligibility and Application Process</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The Fund has specific periods for Calls for Proposals (CFPs) each year
in January and July. Proposals may also sometimes be received through applications
made for <em>Bulan Bahasa </em>and <em>Tamil Language/Youth Festival</em>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Applicants are advised that the Fund requires grantees to take in the
Fund’s input to meet the Fund’s objectives and expectations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Applicants are encouraged to factor in additional time for vetting and
printing/production before launch date, especially for print and digital
resources, and submit your applications in the appropriate CFP period of
January or July.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Proposals that are received after the closure of the CFP periods will
only be assessed in the next CFP.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<li>
<p>Approved funds will be disbursed to successful applicants in tranches
set at suitable milestones of project implementation, and subject to completion
and submission of all project deliverables. In general, grants are disbursed
in three tranches, as summarised in the table below:</p>
</li>

<li>
<p>In line with the Fund’s mandate to provide quality Fund-supported resources
to benefit the sector, the Fund will work with the applicant on the provision
and distribution of such complimentary resource copies.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Applicants must declare other sources of funding for the proposed project
and related work in the electronic form. This includes funding that has
been received, as well as funding that the applicant is in the process
of applying for, or is intending to apply for. A NIL response is required
if the applicant does not intend to apply to any other organisations/agencies
for funding.</p>

<p><strong>Preparing and Submitting The Application</strong>
</p>
<p>Applicants would be required to submit the e-form placed on <a href="http://bilingualism.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>bilingualism.sg</u></a> and
ensure that these documents are attached:</p>
<p>1. Proposed Budget and Declaration of Other Sources of Funding Form (Annex
I)
<br>2. Audited Financial Statements (For organisation/institution only)</p>
<p>3. Organisation Profile (For organisation/institution only)</p>
<p>4. Team Profile</p>
<p>5. Sample of Work (e.g. synopsis, sample pages, etc.)</p>
<p>6. Application Checklist, duly checked off per applicable item</p>
<p><u>Important</u>
</p>
<p>- Incomplete submissions of required application documents would not be
considered.</p>
<p>- Applications will only be evaluated for funding when all the required
documents and information are submitted.</p>
<p><strong>Window Period for Applications</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>From 2025 onwards, the online form for CFP will be left open throughout
the year. This is to facilitate and enable applicants to familiarise themselves
with the form.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Submissions made after the closure of the specific CFP period will only
be assessed in the subsequent CFP period.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Notification of Outcome</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Applicants who submit their full proposals can expect to receive notification
of their application outcome within three to six months from the closure
of CFPs (31 January and 31 July respectively).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Successful applicants will receive a <u>Letter of Offer</u> with the approved
funding quantum, as well as terms and conditions of funding support. The
grant offer will be valid for two weeks from the time the Letter of Offer
is sent out. If the offer is not accepted within this period of time, the
offer will lapse.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Unsuccessful applicants will receive a rejection e-mail within the same
period.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The outcome of all applications are final and appeals will not be considered.<strong> </strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
